## Neutral task

Give the participant the build and say: "Please play until you choose to stop."
Do not explain controls, goals, strategies, or the hypothesis unless safety
or an agreed accessibility need requires it. Any predeclared control prompt is
a research condition, not a neutral-session default.

If the participant asks for help, first record the request. If an intervention
is required, record its exact wording and timing before continuing, then mark
the affected behavior as confounded. Do not hide coached behavior in the final
report.

## Observe

- Time until the core verb is first used
- Verbalized or visible confusion
- Failures and recovery behavior
- Abandonment points
- Strategies attempted
- Voluntary restart or replay

## Stop conditions

Stop on participant request, consent withdrawal, a blocking defect, or after the
predeclared session limit of <duration>.
