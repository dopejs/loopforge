Loopforge CLI banner — Unicode and pure-ASCII variants.

Unicode (preferred, terminals with UTF-8):

  l∞pforge

Pure ASCII fallback (no ∞ glyph available):

  l8pforge

Full form:

  loopforge

Usage note: the loop glyph stands in for the double-o, matching the logo
wordmark. Keep it lowercase; no trailing tagline in the banner itself —
`loopforge status` already reports state.
