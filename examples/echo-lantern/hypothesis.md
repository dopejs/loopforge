## Intended player
First-time players of small keyboard-controlled arcade games.

## Platform
Desktop keyboard, Godot 4 local build.

## Player fantasy
Find beacons by briefly exposing yourself to a pursuer in a dark room.

## Core verb
Toggle the lantern while moving between visible beacons.

## Moment to moment loop
Pulse light to locate a beacon, move toward it, extinguish light to freeze the
pursuer, relight to collect, and repeat until all three are found or caught.

## Hypothesis
After discovering that the pursuer moves only while the lantern is on, a
first-time player will voluntarily use short light pulses instead of leaving
the lantern on continuously.

## Constraints
One screen, keyboard only, three beacons, one pursuer, one short run.

## Non-goals
Progression, procedural content, accounts, production art, and audio.

## Cheapest validation
One neutral external session on the exact tested build, with no explanation of
the light/pursuer relationship before play.

## Keep signals
The participant uses a deliberate short pulse after observing pursuit,
collects a beacon, and restarts voluntarily after failure.

## Kill signals
After two runs, the participant still cannot explain what makes the pursuer
move without coaching.
